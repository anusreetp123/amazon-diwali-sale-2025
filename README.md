# Amazon Diwali Sale
Study trends in product categories and forecast future sales trends, expected revenue, and demand for specific products

## Dataset used
-<a href="https://github.com/anusreetp123/amazon-diwali-sale-2025/blob/main/amazon%20diwali.csv">Dataset</a>

## About The Dataset
It  is a synthetic dataset containing 15,000 e-commerce transactions designed to simulate real-world sales on Amazon India. 
It captures customer purchasing behavior, product details, payment preferences, and feedback patterns across various product categories throughout the year 2025.
This Amazon Diwali Sale dataset is important because it helps us understand real-world customer buying
behavior during a major festival season. It contains detailed information about products, pricing, customer 
reviews, payment methods, and delivery status, which allows us to analyze sales patterns, identify top-selling
categories, understand customer satisfaction, and study return behavior.Using this dataset, we can make 
data-driven decisions to improve marketing strategies, optimize inventory, and increase overall sales
performance.

## Objective
. Analyzing customer demographics (age, gender, occupation, location) and purchase patterns to identify key customer
 segments and their preferences during the festive season
 
. Developing machine learning models (e.g., using Random Forest Regression) to forecast future sales trends, 
 expected revenue, and demand for specific products.
 
. Forecast sales or demand for specific products.

.The goal is to analyze customer purchase patterns, product information, and pricing details to predict the Total 
Sales generated for each product. This helps the company identify the key factors that contribute to higher sales 
during the Amazon Diwali season.
